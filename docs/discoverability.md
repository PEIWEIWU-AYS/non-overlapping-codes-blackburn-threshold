# A quadratic alphabet threshold for non-overlapping codes | 非重叠码的二次字母表阈值

Non-overlapping (cross-bifix-free) codes: quadratic alphabet threshold for Blackburn's construction; counterexample to Conjecture 1 of Stanovnik, Moškon and Mraz; Lean 4 proofs.

本文确定 Blackburn 构造取得最优值的最小字母表阈值的首项系数为 1/2，证明长度 5、6 的精确阈值分别为 4、10，并给出 Stanovnik、Moškon 和 Mraz 猜想 1 的反例。

## Original question and result

[Stanovnik, Moškon and Mraz, *In search of maximum non-overlapping codes*, Conjecture 1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-023-01344-z). Chee proved eventual optimality and [asked for the least threshold and exceptional parameters](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.28776v2#S4). This work proves the leading term of the threshold for growing length, determines it exactly at lengths five and six, and disproves Conjecture 1 at length six on nine letters.
